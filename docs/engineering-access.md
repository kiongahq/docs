# Engineering access

Product documentation covers installing, using, and operating Kionga. Architecture,
implementation details, internal decisions, and contributor processes are maintained
separately in the engineering handbook.

The handbook is restricted to members of the **Kionga GitHub organization**.
Sign in to GitHub with your organization account to open the
[private engineering repository](https://github.com/kiongahq/engineering-docs).
If access is unavailable, contact a Kionga organization owner.
