# Roadmap

This page lists directions Kionga plans to take. It makes no promise about
whether, when or in what form any of them ships. Features that exist today are
in the [release notes](changelog.md).

## Kionga Cloud

**Kionga Cloud** is a Kionga-hosted version of the platform, where Kionga runs
the whole service for you and you don't provide infrastructure. It is planned for
a future version. There is no date, and no commitment to a feature set, pricing or
plans.

Until then, you have two ways to use Kionga:

| Option | Who runs it | Where it runs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self-managed | You | Your infrastructure. See [Choose a deployment](operations/index.md) |
| Managed operations (Enterprise plan) | Kionga's team | Your cloud account, with the same chart. See [Managed operations](operations/managed-operations.md) |

Both keep your data in your own account. Kionga Cloud would be a third option and
would not replace these.
