# Architecture decision records

Technical decisions that shape the system are recorded here in the
[MADR](https://adr.github.io/madr/) format: context, the options considered, the
decision, and its consequences. Records are numbered in the order they were made
and are never deleted; a reversed decision is marked *Superseded* and links to
the record that replaces it.

To propose a decision, copy the MADR template into the next number and open a
pull request.
